# Meridian — Information architecture

State-based navigation and section boundaries. Every feature must map to Focus, Plan, Life, or Capture.

Pair with `user-story-definition.md` (who earns the 15-second clarity moment) and `core-problem-alignment.md` (cognitive burden gate).

---

## Meridian Information Architecture

Meridian uses a **STATE-BASED** architecture instead of a feature-based architecture.

The app is organized around how overloaded adults think during daily life.

Core navigation contains exactly **4 primary sections**:

1. **Focus**
2. **Plan**
3. **Life**
4. **Capture**

---

## 1. Focus

**Purpose:**  
The “what matters now” surface.

**Contains:**

- AI-ranked priorities
- upcoming commitments
- urgent reminders
- contextual awareness
- actionable momentum

**Design goal:**  
Reduce decision fatigue immediately upon app open.

**This is the default landing tab.**

---

## 2. Plan

**Purpose:**  
Forward visibility without overwhelm.

**Contains:**

- schedule
- calendar
- family coordination
- future planning
- upcoming events
- weekly rhythm

**Important:**  
This is **NOT** a traditional enterprise calendar experience.

The emotional tone should feel **calm, breathable, and manageable**.

---

## 3. Life

**Purpose:**  
Reflective life-state awareness.

**Contains:**

- health
- finances
- routines
- energy
- personal progress
- long-term trends

**Important:**  
This section should feel **grounding and insightful**, not analytical or corporate.

---

## 4. Capture

**Purpose:**  
Instant cognitive offloading.

**Contains:**

- voice capture
- quick text input
- reminders
- thoughts
- errands
- notes
- AI parsing

**Critical principle:**  
The user should **not** need to organize information manually during capture.

Meridian handles organization intelligently after entry.

---

## Secondary architecture

Use **sheets / modals** for:

- detail views
- quick add flows
- edits
- contextual insights

**Avoid deep navigation stacks** whenever possible.

---

## Product architecture rule

Every future feature must map naturally into:

- **Focus**
- **Plan**
- **Life**
- or **Capture**

If it does not fit clearly:

- simplify it
- merge it
- or reject it

---

## UX principle

The product should feel like:

> “A calm surface over a complicated life.”
