# Meridian — Core problem alignment

Foundational product principles. Use this to evaluate every feature, screen, interaction, notification, animation, and workflow.

---

## Meridian Core Problem

Busy adults drowning in disconnected apps need one calm, intelligent hub that unifies their schedule, family, health, and finances — and actually makes staying on top of life feel good.

---

## Internal Product Framing

Meridian helps busy adults replace mental overload and app fragmentation with one calm, intelligent system that keeps life moving forward.

---

## Product Category

**Meridian is NOT:**

- a productivity app
- a task manager
- a habit tracker
- a budgeting tool
- a wellness tracker

**Meridian IS:**

A calm intelligence layer for modern adult life.

---

## Core Product Principle

Every feature, screen, interaction, notification, animation, and workflow must **reduce cognitive burden**.

If something increases cognitive load without delivering extraordinary value:

- simplify it
- automate it
- merge it
- or remove it

---

## Emotional Outcome

The user should feel:

- calmer
- clearer
- more grounded
- more capable
- less fragmented

The app should **never** create guilt, pressure, or overwhelm.

---

## UX Philosophy

Meridian should feel:

- intelligent without being complicated
- premium without being flashy
- fast without feeling rushed
- supportive without feeling needy

---

## Design Standard

If a tired parent cannot use the feature one-handed at 9pm with partial attention, the feature is not finished.
