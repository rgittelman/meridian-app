# Meridian — Focus fallback state system

When Focus has little or no data, Meridian shows a **guided momentum state**—not an empty state. Aligns with `design-language.md` (Calm Intelligence + Ambient Motivation), `onboarding-flow.md` (Screen 6 → Focus), and `information-architecture.md` (Focus = “what matters now”).

---

## Core principle

Meridian must **never** feel:

- empty
- unfinished
- broken
- data-dependent
- lifeless

Even with minimal user data, the Focus experience should feel:

- warm
- intelligent
- emotionally supportive
- calming
- already useful

**This is NOT an empty state.**

**This is a GUIDED MOMENTUM STATE.**

---

## Product philosophy

Most apps expose emptiness when data is unavailable.

Meridian should instead provide:

- emotional continuity
- lightweight structure
- ambient intelligence
- soft momentum

The app should always feel **alive and supportive**.

---

## Day-one Focus structure

### 1. Calm welcome header

Use emotionally intelligent greetings.

**Examples:**

- “Good evening, Ryan.”
- “Let’s clear your head a bit.”
- “You’ve already started.”

**Tone:**

- warm
- calm
- grounded
- human

**Avoid:**

- robotic assistant phrasing
- corporate productivity language
- technical AI framing

---

### 2. First momentum card

Immediately surface the user’s **onboarding capture item**.

**Example:**

> “Schedule dentist”

**Include:**

- suggested timing
- lightweight next action
- calm completion affordance

**Goal:**  
Create immediate continuity from onboarding into real usefulness.

---

### 3. Suggested calm wins

Provide lightweight optional suggestions such as:

- “Review tomorrow morning”
- “Connect calendar for clearer planning”
- “Add recurring family routines”

**Rules:**

- optional feeling
- visually lightweight
- low cognitive burden
- never overwhelming

---

### 4. Soft daily structure

Even without integrations, provide emotional day structure:

- Morning
- Afternoon
- Evening

Use gentle placeholder guidance.

**Avoid blank dashboard areas entirely.**

---

### 5. Momentum ring — initial state

**Never** show “0% complete.”

**Instead:**

- partially open warm progress ring
- subtle motion
- label as **“Getting Started”**

**Goal:**  
Communicate that momentum has already begun.

Users should **never** feel like they are starting from failure.

---

### 6. Ambient intelligence hints

Provide subtle emotionally supportive insights.

**Examples:**

- “Capturing thoughts early reduces mental clutter.”
- “Small progress still moves life forward.”

**Tone:**

- calm
- intelligent
- reassuring

**Avoid:**

- AI overexplanation
- robotic coaching
- excessive recommendations

---

### 7. Empty state rule

**Never display:**

- “No tasks yet”
- “Nothing here”
- blank dashboards
- dead-end setup walls

Meridian should always feel:

- responsive
- emotionally present
- quietly intelligent
- momentum-oriented

---

## Long-term applicability

This fallback philosophy applies to:

- first-time users
- low-data users
- disconnected integrations
- offline states
- inactive returning users
- degraded AI states

---

## UX standard

Users should feel:

> “The app already started helping me.”
