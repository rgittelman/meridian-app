# Meridian — User story definition

Foundational user story, emotional transformation, positioning, and behavior standards. Pair with `core-problem-alignment.md` for problem/category framing.

---

## Meridian Core User Story

The app earns its place when a mentally overloaded adult opens Meridian for **15 seconds** and immediately regains clarity about what matters next across work, family, health, and life.

---

## Core Emotional Transformation

**Before Meridian:**

- fragmented
- mentally overloaded
- reactive
- anxious about forgetting things
- juggling disconnected systems

**After Meridian:**

- calmer
- clearer
- grounded
- reassured
- confident about what matters now

---

## Strategic Product Positioning

**Meridian is NOT optimized for:**

- productivity enthusiasts
- extreme customization
- complex organizational systems
- manual planning workflows

**Meridian IS optimized for:**

- overloaded working adults
- interrupted attention spans
- one-handed mobile usage
- rapid context switching
- low cognitive effort

---

## Product Behavior Principles

Meridian should:

- reduce decisions
- reduce app switching
- surface priorities automatically
- intelligently organize information
- minimize setup friction
- reward momentum, not perfection

---

## UX Principle

The user should feel:

> “I don’t need to mentally hold everything anymore.”

---

## Notification Philosophy

Notifications should feel:

- calming
- timely
- helpful
- low-pressure

**Never:**

- guilt-inducing
- spammy
- demanding
- stressful

---

## Product Standard

Every core interaction should be completable within:

- 1 hand
- partial attention
- under 30 seconds
