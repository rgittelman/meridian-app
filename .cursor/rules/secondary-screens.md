# Meridian — Secondary screens & settings system

Progressive depth for trust, emotional safety, and control—without corporate admin energy. Pair with `ai-intelligence.md` (privacy, trust, surfacing levels), `design-language.md`, `user-story-definition.md` (notification philosophy), and `navigation-system.md` (sheets over deep stacks).

---

## Meridian secondary screen philosophy

Secondary systems determine:

- trust
- emotional safety
- retention durability
- AI comfort level
- perceived intelligence

These screens must **never** feel:

- corporate
- administrative
- enterprise-heavy
- configuration obsessed
- technically intimidating

**Core principle:**

> Advanced capability without emotional heaviness.

---

## Progressive depth system

Meridian uses **PROGRESSIVE DEPTH**.

The default experience remains:

- calm
- lightweight
- emotionally simple

More control and depth appear **only when needed**.

## Secondary screen categories

1. **Notifications & Nudges**

4. **People & Household**

---

## 1. Notifications & nudges

Notifications are **emotional interruptions**.

Meridian notifications should:

- reduce stress
- create clarity
- prevent forgetting
- support momentum
- protect recovery time

**Avoid:**

- guilt mechanics
- engagement bait
- fake urgency
- noisy retention tactics

### Notification intensity system

Use **emotional framing** instead of technical labels.

**Options:**

#### Minimal

Only important reminders and conflicts.

#### Balanced

Helpful daily guidance without overload.

#### Active support

More proactive momentum support and timing suggestions.

### Notification tone

Notification copy should feel:

- calm
- brief
- human
- useful
- low-pressure

**Avoid:**

- all caps
- urgency spam
- excessive emoji
- shame language
- “You forgot…”

---

## 2. AI preferences & trust

AI settings must feel **understandable** and **emotionally safe**.

**Avoid:**

- technical AI language
- algorithm terminology
- model configuration concepts

### Example AI controls

#### Focus assistance

Help prioritize what matters most.

#### Timing intelligence

Adjust reminders based on schedule patterns.

#### Recovery protection

Protect lighter windows during overload.

#### Family awareness

Coordinate around household commitments.

**Controls should use:**

- simple toggles
- short explanations
- optional detail sheets

(Aligns with `ai-intelligence.md`: privacy, correction, category opt-outs.)

---

## 3. Integrations

Integrations should be framed as **clarity expansion**, not data syncing.

**Examples:**

| Instead of | Use |
|------------|-----|
| “Connect Google Calendar” | “See your real day in one place.” |
| “Enable Health Permissions” | “Understand energy and recovery patterns.” |

Focus on **emotional outcomes**, not technical permissions.

---

## 4. People & household

The household system should feel:

- lightweight
- warm
- calm
- relationship-oriented

**Avoid:**

- enterprise family management
- parental-control feeling
- logistics-heavy interfaces

### People objects

People may contain:

- name
- relationship
- optional avatar
- recurring commitments
- routines
- shared responsibilities

The UI should remain **visually minimal** and **emotionally warm**.

(Aligns with `focus-screen.md` people-aware schedule and ownership correction.)

---

## 5. Profile & identity

**Avoid:**

- social profile mechanics
- vanity metrics
- follower-style identity systems

The profile experience should feel:

- private
- grounded
- personal
- calm

---

## 6. Recovery & support

Users will:

- fall behind
- stop using the app temporarily
- become overloaded

Meridian must respond with:

- emotional softness
- simplified restart paths
- reduced visible clutter
- recovery-first prioritization

**Avoid:**

- backlog panic
- guilt language
- punishment mechanics
- missed-day pressure

**Example recovery tone:**

> “Let’s get back into rhythm.”

### Recovery UX principle

Returning to Meridian after absence should feel:

**welcoming, not stressful.**

(Aligns with `focus-screen.md` Recovery mode and `design-language.md` momentum-not-perfection.)
