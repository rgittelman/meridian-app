# Meridian — Onboarding flow

First-run experience: **value before effort**. Pair with `meridian-master-prompt.md`, `core-problem-alignment.md`, and `focus-screen.md`.

---

## Philosophy

Onboarding should:

- demonstrate calm intelligence quickly
- avoid forms and configuration walls
- never feel like enterprise setup
- end with the user feeling *lighter*, not *behind*

**Never:** guilt for skipping, long questionnaires, permission spam before value.

---

## Six screens (target)

| # | Purpose | Emotional goal |
|---|---------|----------------|
| 1 | Welcome | “This is different — calm, not hustle.” |
| 2 | Problem mirror | “They understand my mental load.” |
| 3 | Quiet intelligence | “AI helps without performing.” |
| 4 | Life Objects demo | “I don’t have to classify everything.” |
| 5 | Focus preview | “I’ll know what matters when I open the app.” |
| 6 | Gentle start | “I can begin without finishing setup.” |

---

## Rules

- **Show Focus-like clarity** before asking for calendar, household, or integrations.
- **Defer** deep settings to `secondary-screens.md` paths.
- **Optional** household and calendar connect — never block first value.
- Respect **9pm Tired Parent** pacing: large type, one decision per screen, skip always visible.
- On completion, land on **Focus** (or guided momentum per `focus-fallback-state.md`).

---

## Recovery

If the user abandons mid-flow, resume softly — no “you’re almost done” pressure.
