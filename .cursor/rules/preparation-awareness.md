# Preparation Awareness

## Preparation Awareness Is Event-Centric

The upcoming commitment is always the primary object.

Preparation context exists only to support the commitment.

If preparation awareness makes Focus or Plan feel heavier than the events themselves, it has failed.

Meridian should help users arrive prepared without turning preparation into another task list.

## Focus

1. **Commitments first** — Schedule / coming-up awareness appears before any prep section.
2. **Prep follows** — PrepAwarenessSection sits below ScheduleStrip, never above Focus Stack commitments surfacing.
3. **Light mass** — Show at most 1–2 prep cards; summarize additional threads calmly rather than listing them.
4. **Event-first cards** — Primary label is the event (optionally prefixed with person context); timing and attribution are secondary; prep readiness body is subordinate; connected items are footnotes.

## Plan

1. **Calendar events lead** — Hard calendar commitments render first within each day.
2. **Captured intentions are subordinate** — Promoted captures group under a soft day label (“Captured intentions”) below calendar events for that day.
3. **No inline task actions** — Hold / Handled and similar actions belong in detail sheets, not on the Plan timeline.

## Product boundary

Meridian is not a task manager. Prep awareness surfaces readiness and connected context — not a checklist competing with what is coming.
