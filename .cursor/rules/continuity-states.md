# Meridian — Continuity states specification

Meridian uses **continuity states**, not traditional empty states. The product stays alive, supportive, and calmly intelligent when data, connectivity, AI confidence, or user activity are limited. Pair with `focus-fallback-state.md` (Focus-specific guided momentum), `design-language.md` (Ambient Motivation + loading/completion), and `ai-intelligence.md` (wrong gracefully, surfacing levels).

---

## Meridian continuity state philosophy

Meridian does **not** use traditional empty states.

Meridian uses **CONTINUITY STATES**.

The product should always feel:

- alive
- responsive
- emotionally supportive
- calmly intelligent

**even when:**

- data is unavailable
- AI confidence is low
- internet is unstable
- users are inactive
- systems are loading

---

## Core principle

**Never** visually communicate:

> “There is nothing here.”

**Instead** communicate:

> “We’re still helping.”

---

## Empty state philosophy

Empty states should:

- reduce pressure
- preserve emotional continuity
- gently guide momentum
- feel warm and intentional

**Avoid:**

- feature advertisements
- giant blank spaces
- setup walls
- emotionally cold placeholders

---

## Focus continuity state

When Focus load is light, use:

- breathing room
- gentle encouragement
- calm reassurance
- lightweight guidance

**Examples:**

- “A lighter day today.”
- “Nothing urgent right now.”
- “You’ve cleared some space.”

The screen should feel **peaceful**, not empty.

(See `focus-fallback-state.md` for day-one structure: welcome, momentum card, calm wins, soft daily structure, “Getting Started” ring.)

---

## Capture continuity state

Capture should reinforce **emotional release**.

**Examples:**

- “Get it out of your head.”
- “What’s taking up space today?”
- “Throw thoughts here. Meridian organizes later.”

**Goal:**  
Teach cognitive offloading emotionally.

---

## Life continuity state

**Never** show empty analytics dashboards.

**Instead:**

- soft trend placeholders
- lightweight momentum framing
- future-oriented guidance

**Examples:**

- “Small routines become momentum.”
- “Life patterns become clearer over time.”

---

## Loading state philosophy

Loading should feel:

- stable
- calm
- intentional
- spatially consistent

**Avoid:**

- abrupt layout shifts
- floating spinners
- jittery loading behavior
- empty white screens

---

## Skeleton system

**Use:**

- soft shimmer
- preserved layout structure
- low-contrast placeholders
- stable spacing hierarchy

The app should feel **temporarily settling**, not broken.

---

## AI loading behavior

**Avoid:**

- “AI thinking…” theatrics
- chatbot typing animations
- dramatic intelligence indicators

**Prefer:**

- subtle refinement
- calm fade-ins
- contextual placeholder transitions

The AI should feel **ambient and natural**.

---

## Error state philosophy

Errors should feel:

- calm
- recoverable
- emotionally safe
- human

**Avoid:**

- technical jargon
- error codes
- blame language
- panic tone

**Preferred examples:**

- “Couldn’t load that right now.”
- “Still syncing a few things.”
- “Try again in a moment.”

---

## Offline principles

Offline behavior should preserve:

- recent Focus state
- recent Life Objects
- cached schedule awareness
- local capture functionality

**Critical rule:**

> **Capture must NEVER fail offline.**

---

## AI uncertainty behavior

When confidence is low, **soften certainty** instead of pretending confidence.

**Examples:**

- “This might belong tomorrow.”
- “Looks related to your board meeting.”
- “Want to group these together?”

**Goal:**  
Create **humble intelligence**.

(Aligns with `ai-intelligence.md` people-awareness confidence levels and `focus-screen.md` medium/low ownership display.)

---

## Recovery state philosophy

After user inactivity:

- reduce visible overwhelm
- simplify Focus
- remove guilt
- reduce urgency pressure
- prioritize restart momentum

**Avoid:**

- overdue explosions
- backlog panic
- missed-day pressure
- streak punishment

(Aligns with `focus-screen.md` Recovery mode and `secondary-screens.md` Recovery & Support.)

---

## Success state philosophy

Success feedback should feel:

- satisfying
- calm
- emotionally grounding

**Use:**

- soft haptics
- subtle ring completion
- elegant motion
- restrained reinforcement

**Avoid:**

- loud celebrations
- confetti spam
- dopamine overload
- arcade reward systems

(Aligns with `design-language.md` completion interaction and forbidden motivation patterns.)

---

## Emotional trust rule

Every continuity state must preserve:

- calmness
- trust
- competence
- emotional safety

**even when** systems are uncertain or incomplete.

---

## Platform variants

### iOS

- native skeleton rhythm
- spring easing
- translucent loading layers
- native haptic cadence

### Android

- Material 3 skeleton treatment
- edge-to-edge continuity
- restrained motion timing
- Android-native loading behavior

---

## Final UX standard

Meridian should **never emotionally disappear** when uncertainty appears.
