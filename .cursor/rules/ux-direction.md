# Meridian UX Direction

## What Meridian Is
A thoughtful life companion — calm, intentional, emotionally supportive.

## What Meridian Is NOT
A power-user productivity dashboard. Not Arc. Not Linear. Not enterprise.

## Inspiration Blend
Todoist (clarity, fast scanning) · Things 3 (hierarchy, elegance) · Microsoft To Do (softness, friendliness) · Tiimo (emotional design, stress reduction) · Any.do (momentum, mobile-first)

## Emotional Target
"This app helps me feel more organized and mentally lighter."

## UX Principles

### 1. Softer and Warmer
- Gentle contrast, not harsh black-on-white
- Soft surfaces with warm shadows
- Comfortable spacing — never cramped
- Human feeling, not clinical

### 2. Things 3 Hierarchy
- Typography rhythm over visual noise
- Spacing creates grouping (not dividers)
- Visual calm and digestibility
- Elegant section headers

### 3. Todoist Clarity
- Fast scanning, obvious urgency
- Clear organization at a glance
- Minimal cognitive effort
- Information hierarchy through weight/color, not chrome

### 4. Microsoft To Do Softness
- Rounded surfaces (16-20px radii)
- Gentle sectioning through background tints
- Friendly spacing between elements
- Less rigid, more flowing

### 5. Tiimo Emotional Design
- Reduce stress, never overwhelm
- Progress feels encouraging
- Subtle emotional reinforcement
- Overdue items are gentle nudges, not alarms

### 6. Any.do Momentum
- Quick interactions, low friction
- Smooth completion animations
- Lightweight task flow
- Mobile-first ergonomics

## Today Screen Philosophy
- **Today**: focused, actionable, most prominent
- **Tomorrow**: light anticipation, slightly softer
- **This Week / Later**: quiet, reduced emphasis
- **No Date**: gentle parking area
- **Completed**: satisfying but unobtrusive

## Visual Rules
- Prefer spacing over dividers/lines
- Softer shadows (low opacity, wide spread)
- Intentional whitespace
- Typography hierarchy through weight and size, not color extremes
- Rounded card edges (16-20px)
- No hard edges or heavy borders

## Interaction Rules
- Buttery swipe gestures with soft spring curves
- Satisfying completion feedback (scale + check animation)
- Subtle haptics on meaningful actions
- Lightweight transitions (150-300ms, ease-out)

## Avoid
- Enterprise dashboard aesthetic
- Developer-centric labels (e.g., "TASKS", "REMINDERS" in all-caps)
- Harsh UI chrome or heavy borders
- Dense information stacking
- Over-minimal tech aesthetic
- Aggressive overdue styling
